A little more clarity

Good questions.
Clear answers.

Does using this calculator affect my credit?

No. It performs a calculation in your browser. There is no credit check or application.

What does the estimate include?

The estimate uses vehicle price minus down payment, the APR entered and the selected loan term. Taxes, dealership fees, insurance and optional products are excluded.

Can I use a trade-in as a down payment?

Trade-in equity may contribute toward a purchase. Any remaining loan balance and the final vehicle valuation would need to be confirmed by the dealership.

Is financing guaranteed?

No. This prototype makes no credit decision or financing offer. A lender would determine eligibility and final terms.
